# PDFs and voice notes

## PDFs

**Add a PDF** by dragging it onto a folder in the Files tab, or by pasting or dropping it into a note (which also shows it there). Click a PDF in the Files tab to read it.

The PDF viewer works the same on the computer and the iPhone (where browsers usually show only a PDF's first page):

- **Pages**: scroll through them, or open the page thumbnails to jump. **Go to page…** in the palette goes to a page number.
- **Find**: `Ctrl+F` (or **Find in PDF**) highlights every match; `Enter` goes to the next one.
- **Zoom**: the zoom buttons and sizes at the top, `Ctrl`+scroll on a computer, or pinch on the phone. **Zoom to page width** and **Zoom to whole page** are in the palette.
- **Copy text**: select it as on a web page.
- **Dark pages**: in the dark theme pages are shown dark too. The **Dark pages** button turns that off on this device, which suits PDFs full of photos.
- The viewer remembers the page you were reading, per device.
- A password-protected PDF asks for its password.
- The buttons at the top also **Download** the PDF or open it in the browser's own viewer.

**Link to a page**: `[[Manual.pdf#page=12]]` opens the PDF at page 12. **Copy link to current PDF page** (palette, while reading) copies such a link for you.

**Show a PDF in a note**: `![[Manual.pdf]]`, or one page with `![[Manual.pdf#page=3]]`. Add `#height=400` to change the height of the frame.

Search finds the text inside PDFs (scanned pages without text excepted), and a result opens the PDF at the matching page.

## Voice notes

Record audio straight into a note:

1. Put the cursor where the recording should go.
2. **Phone**: tap the microphone on the keyboard bar. **Computer**: run **Record voice note** from the palette, or `/voice` in the note.
3. A small panel shows the time and the sound level. **Pause** and **Resume** as needed, then **Done**. **Discard** throws the recording away.

The recording is saved as an audio file in your vault (named like `Recording 20261102143005.m4a`) and plays right in the note. The first time, the browser asks to use the microphone; allow it for Voyager.

### Getting the words written out

If the server has a transcription service set up (ask whoever runs it), the panel shows a **Transcribe** checkbox. With it ticked, the text of the recording appears under it as a quote a moment after **Done**, and search finds the recording by its words. To transcribe an older recording, put the cursor on its line and run **Transcribe recording on this line**. Whether new recordings are transcribed is a per-device switch, in the panel and under **Settings › This device › Transcribe voice notes**.
