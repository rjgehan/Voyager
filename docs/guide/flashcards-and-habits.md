# Flashcards and habits

## Flashcards

Write questions and answers in any note, and NotesApp quizzes you on them, showing each card again just before you would forget it (spaced repetition). The cards stay in your notes, written the same way as for Obsidian's Spaced Repetition plugin.

### Write cards

Tag the note `#flashcards` (in the text or in its tags property), then write cards in any of these forms:

| Write | Cards |
|---|---|
| `Capital of France::Paris` | One card: question, then answer |
| `Paris:::Capital of France` | Two cards, one each way |
| The question on some lines, a line with just `?`, then the answer | One card with several lines |
| The same with `??` | Two cards, one each way |
| `The ==mitochondria== is the powerhouse of the cell` | A fill-in-the-blank card for each highlighted part |

A card ends at the next blank line. Nested tags make decks within decks: `#flashcards/spanish/verbs`. A line holding only tags starts a new deck for the cards below it.

### Review

Open **Flashcards** (ribbon or drawer). It lists your decks with three counts: new cards (blue), cards being learned (orange) and cards due (green).

1. **Study now**, or tap a deck to study just that deck.
2. Read the question, try to remember, then tap the card or **Show answer**.
3. Say how well you remembered: **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. Each button shows when the card will come back.

On a computer: `Space` or `Enter` shows the answer (and then answers Good), `1` to `4` answer Again to Easy, `U` undoes, `E` opens the card in its note, `S` skips it, and `Esc` ends the session. The **⋯** menu during a review has **Edit in note**, **Skip for now** and **Reset progress**.

- **Review flashcards in this note** (palette) studies the open note's cards, without the daily limits.
- **Statistics** (the chart button on the Flashcards page) shows how many cards you answered, your streak, how much you remember, and what is due in the coming 30 days.
- **Flashcard settings** (the gear on the Flashcards page) change the tags that make decks (or use folders as decks), the daily limits and how hard the schedule pushes you.

Editing a card's question a little keeps its progress; your answers are saved in the vault and shared by all your devices, and answers given offline are sent when you are back online.

## Habits

Track things you want to do regularly, with one tap a day, streaks and a calendar of how it went.

### Add a habit

Open **Habits** (ribbon or drawer) and choose **New habit** (or run **Create new habit** in the palette). Give it:

- a **name** (it becomes a note in the `Habits` folder);
- **how often**: every day, weekdays, weekends, chosen days, or a number of times a week;
- a **colour**;
- how many **missed days a streak survives**, if any.

### Check in

- On the **Habits** page, tap a habit's round button to mark it done today. The strip of the last seven days at the top lets you check in a day you forgot.
- In a daily note, the **Habits** tab of the right sidebar lists the habits for that note's day, with the same buttons.
- Or give the daily note a property named after the habit, such as `exercise: true`; a number above 0 counts too (`water: 6`).

Tap a habit to see its current and longest streak, how often you kept it over the last 30 days, and a year-long calendar. Habits are notes, so they sync and keep their history like any note, and they work with Obsidian's Habit Tracker 21 plugin.
