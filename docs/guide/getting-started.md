# Getting started

NotesApp is a notes app that runs on your own server. Your notes are ordinary Markdown files in a folder on that server, called the **vault**. Every device opens the same vault, everything you type is saved as you type, and every change is kept in the note's [history](history-and-trash.md).

## The screen on a computer

From left to right:

- **The ribbon**, a thin strip of icons: quick switcher, search, today's daily note, insert template, new note, quick capture and the command palette at the top; then pages such as Graph, Flashcards, Habits, Calendar and the task pages; and at the bottom **Help**, **Trash**, **Settings** and the light/dark switch. Hover over an icon to see its name and key.
- **The left sidebar**, with tabs for **Files**, **Search**, **Bookmarks**, **Tags**, **Recent files** and **Properties**. The button at the top of the ribbon shows and hides it.
- **The note** in the middle, with a row of tabs above it (see [Tabs and split panes](tabs-and-splits.md)).
- **The right sidebar**, with tabs for the open note: **Backlinks**, **Outline**, **History**, **Local graph**, **Habits**, **Ask**, **Calendar** and **Tasks linking to this note**.
- **The status bar** at the bottom: whether the app is connected (**Live**), whether everything is saved, how many notes link here, and the word count.

> [!NOTE]
> On a tablet-sized window the ribbon becomes a narrow rail and both sidebars open over the note instead of beside it.

## The screen on a phone

The phone shows one note at a time. The bar at the top has:

- **☰** opens the drawer: **New note**, **Today** and **Capture** buttons, the pages (Calendar, Task lists, Flashcards and so on), the sidebar tabs (Files, Search, Bookmarks, Tags, Recent files, Properties), and at the bottom **Settings**, **Trash**, **Help** and the theme switch.
- The note's name, with a coloured dot for the connection.
- **+** for [quick capture](capture-and-clipper.md).
- The magnifier for the quick switcher.
- A number: how many tabs are open. Tap it to switch tabs.
- **⋯** for the command palette, the note's panels (Backlinks, Outline, History, Local graph), Bookmark, Rename, Move, Delete, Insert template, Today, Help, the theme and Reload.

The right sidebar's panels open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; drag its handle down to close it. More in [Phone tips](phone.md).

## Make your first note

1. Press `Ctrl+Alt+N`, or click the new-note icon in the ribbon or in the Files tab (phone: **☰ › New note**).
2. Type the note's name. It shows at the top as the note's title.
3. Press `Enter` to move into the note, and write.

There is no Save button: the note saves as you type. The status bar (phone: the pill next to the title) says **Saved** or **Unsaved**, and **Offline** when the server cannot be reached; your edits are then kept on the device and saved when the connection returns.

> [!TIP]
> To rename a note, click its title at the top and type. Links to it in other notes are updated for you.

## The two keys that find everything

- **Quick switcher, `Ctrl+O`**: type a few letters of a note's name and press `Enter` to open it. If no note has that name, the last row offers to create it (or press `Shift+Enter`). With nothing typed it lists your recent files. Phone: the magnifier in the top bar.
- **Command palette, `Ctrl+P`**: every action in the app, by name. Type a few letters ("table", "today", "rename", "help") and press `Enter`. Each row shows its key, so the palette is also how to learn the keys. Phone: **⋯ › Command palette**, or the ⌘ button on the keyboard toolbar.

## Folders and files

The **Files** tab lists your folders and notes.

- **Make a folder**: the new-folder button at the top of the Files tab, or right-click a folder and choose **New folder here**.
- **Move a note**: drag it onto a folder (computer), or use **Move to…** from its menu. The dialog tells you how many links will be updated.
- **Menus**: right-click a file or folder on a computer; on a phone, long-press it or tap its **⋯**. A folder's menu creates new notes, spreadsheets, drawings and canvases in it, a note from a template, and subfolders; a file's menu also has Rename, Bookmark, Open in new tab, Open to the right, Copy link, File history, Download and Delete.
- **Add files**: drag files from your computer onto a folder, or paste and drop images into a note (see [Images and files](writing.md#images-and-files)).
- **Sort**: the sort button switches between by name and by most recently changed.

Deleted notes go to the [trash](history-and-trash.md#the-trash), so nothing is lost by accident.

## Working on several devices

Open the app on your phone and your PC at the same time and a change on one shows on the other within a second or two. The dot next to the connection status is green while the app is connected (**Live**). If two devices change the same note at the same time, NotesApp never quietly overwrites either version: it asks you which to keep (see [When two devices disagree](history-and-trash.md#when-two-devices-disagree)).
