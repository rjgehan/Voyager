# Tabs and split panes

Keep several notes open at once, and show two or more side by side on a bigger screen.

## Tabs

The row above the note shows its tabs. Opening a note normally replaces the note in the current tab; to open it in a **new tab** instead:

- `Ctrl`+click it in the Files tab, bookmarks, recent files, search results, backlinks or the outline, or middle-click it;
- in a note, `Ctrl+Shift`+click a link (a plain `Ctrl`+click follows the link in the same tab);
- in the quick switcher, press `Ctrl+Enter` instead of `Enter`;
- **Open in new tab** in a file's menu, or **Open current file in new tab** in the palette.

**+** at the end of the row (or `Ctrl+Alt+T`, or a double-click on the empty part of the row) opens an empty tab with your recent files.

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Close a tab | Its **×**, a middle-click, or `Ctrl+Alt+W` |
| Bring back a closed tab | **Reopen closed tab** in the palette or a tab's menu (`Ctrl+Shift+T` in the desktop app) |
| Go to the next or previous tab | `Ctrl+Alt+PageDown` or `Ctrl+Alt+PageUp` |
| Reorder tabs | Drag them |
| Keep a tab | **Pin** it from its menu (right-click the tab). A pinned tab shows a pin instead of **×**, is not closed by Close tab, Close others or Close tabs to the right, and the notes you open from it open in a new tab. |

Each tab remembers its own back and forward history (the arrows at the top left of the note, or `Ctrl+Alt+←` and `Ctrl+Alt+→`). Your tabs are kept per device and come back when you reopen the app, with their history: the arrows still go back through the notes you had opened in that tab.

> [!NOTE]
> In a web browser, `Ctrl+Shift+T` reopens the browser's own last closed tab, not NotesApp's. Use the palette, or give **Reopen closed tab** another key under Settings › Hotkeys.

## Split panes

Show two notes side by side, or one above the other:

- **Split right** or **Split down**: the buttons at the right end of the tab row, a tab's menu, or the palette.
- **Drag a tab** to the left, right, top or bottom edge of a pane to split it there; drop it in the middle of a pane, or on its tab row, to move it there.
- Drag the line between panes to resize them.
- `Ctrl+Alt+]` and `Ctrl+Alt+[` move between panes. **Close pane** is in the palette and the tab row.

The sidebars (backlinks, outline, history…) follow the pane you are working in. The same note can be open in two panes, and both stay in step as you type. Spreadsheets, drawings, canvases and encrypted notes show in one pane at a time; the other pane offers **Show it here**.

## On the phone

The phone shows one note at a time. The number in the top bar is how many tabs are open: tap it to see them all, switch to one, close one with its **×**, or open a **New tab**. Splits you made on a computer come back when the window is wide enough.
