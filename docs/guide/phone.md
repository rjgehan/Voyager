# Phone tips

NotesApp is made to work well on an iPhone. These tips are for the iPhone; other phones work much the same.

## Add NotesApp to the Home Screen

1. Open NotesApp in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

NotesApp then opens from its own icon, full screen like an app, and it can show notes you opened recently even without a connection. Reminders by notification on an iPhone (Web Push, iOS 16.4 or later) also need the Home Screen app: subscribe it under **Settings › Reminders**.

## Getting around

- **☰** (top left) opens the drawer: **New note**, **Today**, **Capture**, the pages (Calendar, tasks, Flashcards, Habits…), the Files, Search, Bookmarks, Tags, Recent files and Properties tabs, and **Settings**, **Trash**, **Help** and the theme at the bottom.
- **⋯** (top right) has the command palette, the note's panels (Backlinks, Outline, History, Local graph), Bookmark, Rename, Move, Delete, Insert template, Today, Help, the theme and **Reload**. (Pulling the page down does not reload it; use Reload.)
- Panels open as a **sheet from the bottom**; drag its handle down, or swipe down from its top, to close it. The sheet's tabs reach the other panels, such as Calendar, Habits and Ask.
- The **number** in the top bar is how many tabs are open; tap it to switch.
- The **magnifier** opens the quick switcher; type a name, or choose **Search in notes for "…"** to search inside notes.
- **Long-press** a file, folder, bookmark, table cell or canvas card for its menu (files, folders and bookmarks also have a **⋯** button).
- The edge swipe that goes back closes the drawer, a sheet or a dialog first.

## The keyboard bar

While you write, a bar of buttons sits above the keyboard. Swipe it sideways for more:

**/** (insert anything), `[[ ]]` (link), **#** (tag), bold, italic, heading, list, checkbox, link, table, inline code, divider line, outdent, indent, undo, redo, **follow link**, the **command palette**, **insert image** (from Photos or the camera), **record voice note**, and **hide keyboard**.

In a table cell it shows **Previous cell** and **Next cell** and hides the buttons that change whole lines.

There is no toolbar over selected text on the phone; select text and use the keyboard bar, or the iPhone's own menu.

## Writing on the phone

- **Follow a link**: tap it. To change it, long-press it.
- **Tick a checkbox**: tap it.
- **Edit an image, callout or formula**: tap it to see its text; on embeds, tap the small `</>` button in their corner.
- **Tables**: tap a cell to type; long-press a cell for its menu. In a formula, tap the cells to put their names in; tap a second cell to make a range (see [Tables and formulas](tables-and-formulas.md#typing-a-formula-without-typing-cell-names)).
- **Spreadsheets**: tap a cell to select it, tap again to type in the bar above the grid.
- **Drawings, canvases and PDFs**: pinch to zoom, drag to move around.

## Offline

The phone keeps a copy of the last 100 notes you opened, so they open without a connection (Settings › This device › **Keep recent notes for offline reading**, on by default). You can edit them offline: a banner says you are offline, and your changes are saved to the server as soon as the connection returns. If the same note changed on another device meanwhile, you choose which version to keep (see [When two devices disagree](history-and-trash.md#when-two-devices-disagree)). Quick captures made offline wait and are sent later too.

Notes you have not opened on the phone recently cannot be opened offline.

## Capture from anywhere

- **+** in the top bar opens [quick capture](capture-and-clipper.md): a line, a link or a photo, into your Inbox or today's note.
- To send things from other apps (Safari, Photos, Notes) through the **Share** sheet, set up the iPhone Shortcuts described in **Settings › Quick capture** and **Settings › Web clipper**. See [Quick capture and the web clipper](capture-and-clipper.md).

## Good to know

- Spell check and autocorrect (which on the iPhone also controls smart quotes and dashes) are switches under **Settings › This device**.
- Settings under **This device** apply only to the device you set them on, so the phone and the PC can differ.
- Vim keys are a computer-only setting; they are never active on a touch screen.
