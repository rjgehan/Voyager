# Canvas

A canvas is an endless board where you lay out cards, notes, pictures and web links, group them, and join them with arrows. It is good for mind maps, project overviews and planning. Canvases use the same `.canvas` format as Obsidian, so they open in both.

## Make a canvas

Run **Create new canvas** from the palette, or right-click a folder and choose **New canvas here**. To make one and show it inside a note, type `/canvas`.

## Add things

- **A card**: double-click (or double-tap) an empty spot, or use **Add card** in the toolbar at the bottom. Type in it; Markdown, `[[links]]` and `#tags` work, with the same suggestions as in a note.
- **A note or file from your vault**: **Add note or file from the vault** in the toolbar, or drag it from the Files tab onto the canvas (computer). A note shows its content.
- **A picture, audio or video**: **Add image or media**, or drop a file from your computer.
- **A web page**: **Add web page** and paste its address. It shows the address with an **Open** button.

Right-clicking an empty spot (phone: long-press) offers the same, placed where you pressed, plus **Paste**, **Select all** and **Zoom to fit**.

## Arrange

- **Move**: drag a card. Cards snap to a grid; hold `Alt` while dragging to place them freely.
- **Resize**: drag a card's edge or corner.
- **Select several**: drag a box around them on an empty spot (mouse), or `Shift`+click them.
- **Edit a card**: double-click it. Double-clicking a note or link card opens it instead.
- **Connect**: point at a card and drag from one of the four dots on its sides to another card. Dropping the arrow on an empty spot creates a new card there, already connected. Double-click an arrow to give it a label.
- **Group**: select cards, right-click and choose **Create group**. Moving a group moves everything in it. Double-click its name to rename it.

The menu of a card (right-click, or long-press on the phone) also has **Duplicate**, **Copy**, **Cut**, **Bring to front**, **Send to back**, colours, **Zoom to selection**, **Delete**, and for a text card **Convert to note…**, which turns it into a note of its own. An arrow's menu edits its label, reverses its direction or changes its colour.

## Move around

| | Computer | Phone and iPad |
|---|---|---|
| Move the view | Scroll, or drag with the middle button, or hold `Space` and drag | Drag an empty spot with one finger |
| Zoom | `Ctrl`+scroll, or pinch on a trackpad | Pinch |
| See everything | **Zoom to fit** in the toolbar | **Zoom to fit** in the toolbar |

The zoom percentage in the toolbar resets the zoom to 100% when clicked. `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Shift+Z` undo and redo, as do the toolbar's buttons.
